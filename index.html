Scholarly Contributions

Research Publications (11 Papers)

Peer-reviewed articles published across international mathematics journals, featuring pioneering results in S-Valued Graphs, Chromaticity, and Sequence Spaces.

Graph Theory 2017

Colouring of S-Valued Graphs

Shriprakash, T.V.G. and Chandramouleeswaran, M.

International Journal of Pure and Applied Mathematics (IJPAM), Vol. 112(5), pp. 123–129 (2017)

Investigates proper vertex colorings of graphs whose vertex/edge weights take values from an algebraic semiring S, establishing conditions under which valid colorings exist.

Graph Theory 2017

K-Colourable on S-Valued Graphs

Shriprakash, T.V.G. and Chandramouleeswaran, M.

International Journal of Mathematical Sciences and Engineering Applications (IJMSEA), Vol. 11(1), pp. 151–157 (2017)

Extends the concept of k-colorability to graphs weighted with semiring elements, characterizing the boundary conditions for k-partite partitionability under algebraic valuations.

Graph Theory 2017

Chromatic Number of Some S-Valued Graphs

Shriprakash, T.V.G. and Chandramouleeswaran, M.

International Journal of Engineering Science Invention (IJESI), Vol. 6(8), pp. 29–33 (2017)

Computes exact chromatic numbers for fundamental graph families (cycles, wheels, complete bipartite graphs) evaluated under diverse semiring structures.

Graph Theory 2017

Critical Graphs of S-Valued Graphs

Shriprakash, T.V.G. and Chandramouleeswaran, M.

International Journal of Current Advanced Research (IJCAR), Vol. 6(8), pp. 5654–5656 (2017)

Defines critical S-valued graphs whose chromatic properties change upon the removal of any vertex or edge, establishing structural minimal invariants.

Graph Theory 2018

Edge Coloring of S-Valued Graphs

Shriprakash, T.V.G. and Chandramouleeswaran, M.

Proceedings of ICADM’18, Madurai Kamaraj University, pp. 434–447 (2018) | ISSN: 2348-6800

Comprehensive treatise presented at ICADM’18 formalizing the theory of edge colorings in S-valued graphs and defining proper chromatic indices.

Graph Theory 2018

Line Graph of S-Valued Graphs

Shriprakash, T.V.G. and Chandramouleeswaran, M.

International Journal of Mathematical Sciences and Engineering Applications (IJMSEA), Vol. 12(1), pp. 113–122 (2018)

Explores line graph constructions L(G) where edges carry semiring valuations, mapping edge colorings in original graphs to vertex colorings in dual structures.

Graph Theory 2018

Vizing’s Theorem for S-Valued Graphs

Shriprakash, T.V.G. and Chandramouleeswaran, M.

International Journal of Trend in Research and Development (IJTRD), Vol. 5(2), pp. 708–715 (2018)

Proves the generalization of Vizing’s celebrated Edge Coloring Theorem for S-valued graphs, bounding the chromatic index in terms of maximum degree Δ and semiring valuation properties.

Sequence Spaces 2015

The Triple Almost Lacunary 3 Γ Spaces Sequence Defined by a Modulus Function

International Journal of Applied Engineering Research (IJAER), Vol. 10(80) (2015) | ISSN: 0973-4562

Introduces new classes of triple almost lacunary sequence spaces generated by a modulus function, investigating topological linearity and dual spaces.
